About ChargeWay

ChargeWay is an EV charging station that provides convenient and reliable charging services, including fast charging, multiple charging connector options, online booking, and a waiting area with free Wi-Fi.

ChargeWay will be located at a designated portion of the parking area of Robinsons Place Antipolo, along Sumulong Highway corner Circumferential Road, Barangay Dela Paz, Antipolo City, Rizal.

ChargeWay is designed to accommodate different EV models and brands, including BYD, Tesla, VinFast, and Jetour, depending on the vehicle's compatible charging connector.

Charging Services

ChargeWay will offer multiple charging connector options, including Type 2/CCS2, NACS/TP301, CCS1/NACS, and GB/T, depending on vehicle compatibility.

Customers can access the ChargeWay online booking website through the link on its Facebook page. They can check available slots, select their preferred date and time, and complete the required booking details. A confirmation email will be sent once the reservation is successfully confirmed.

ChargeWay will operate daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM. The online booking website may be accessed through the Facebook page.

Technical Specifications

Our charging machine has a total capacity of 240 kW with 4 charging ports, each providing 60 kW of power.

We support multiple EV brands with specific charging ports: BYD (Type 2 / CCS2), Tesla (NACS / TP301), VinFast (CCS1 / NACS), and Jetour (GB/T).

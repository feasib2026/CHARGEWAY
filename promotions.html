Loyalty Rewards Program

After every completed transaction, you'll receive a loyalty card to track your rewards and transactions. The more you charge, the more you save!

How It Works:

  • Receive a loyalty card after each completed charging transaction
  • Your loyalty card tracks your transaction count and rewards progress
  • Reach transaction milestones to unlock exclusive discounts
  • Show your loyalty card at the station to claim your rewards

Reward Milestones:

  • 5th Transaction: 30% discount on your charging session
  • 10th Transaction: 45% discount on your charging session
  • Continue Charging: Additional rewards and special offers for loyal customers
